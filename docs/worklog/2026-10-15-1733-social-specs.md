- **Universal 9:16 box:** top 14%, bottom 40%, left 96 px, right 240 px. Left widened from the 6% proposal because Shorts (96 px)
  and TikTok (80 px) side margins exceed 65 px; the test caught it.
- **Safe zone (official diagram):** 14% top, 6% sides, 35% bottom, 40% in the right-hand 21% strip (Reels ads with labels).
  Dataset stores the conservative 40% over the full width plus `bottom_standard_pct` and `notch`.
- **Durations kept conservative where official pages conflict:** Stories ads 120 s (help page says 60 min), Facebook Reels 90 s
  (Meta says no length limit). Conflicts are written in the records.
